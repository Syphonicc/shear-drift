Why RRT breaks where blood reverses

At each point of the wall, forward and backward shear partly cancel over a heartbeat. Time-averaged shear (TAWSS) adds them up, so errors stay proportionate. Residence time (RRT) depends on what survives the cancellation, so the same error is magnified.
